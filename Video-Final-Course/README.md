## 🎥 VLSI System Design Workshop — Final Video Presentation

This video marks the completion of my **10-day VLSI System Design Workshop**, where I had the opportunity to explore the complete journey from **RTL design to physical implementation** using open-source EDA tools.

Throughout these ten days, I gained valuable hands-on experience with Verilog HDL, simulation, logic synthesis, timing analysis, floorplanning, placement, clock tree synthesis, and routing. More importantly, this workshop helped me connect theoretical concepts with practical applications and develop a deeper understanding of the semiconductor design process.
